6. Cookies and local storage

You can accept or decline cookies in the banner on your first visit. You can change your decision or withdraw consent at any time via the “Cookie settings” link at the bottom of the Website; you can also delete cookies in your browser settings.

To do so, send a request to maximilian.antipenko@gmail.com stating the name and phone number given in your request.
