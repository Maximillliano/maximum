6. Cookies and local storage

Cookies are small files saved in your browser. The Website uses:

  • Necessary (work without consent): storing your cookie choice (12 months) and a flag showing that the intro animation has already been displayed. They do not identify you and are not passed to third parties.
  • Analytics (loaded only after your consent in the banner): Yandex Metrica (Yandex LLC, Russia) and Google Analytics (Google, USA). They collect information about visits to the Website: pages viewed, traffic source, device and browser type, approximate location, clicks and link transitions. Cookies of the _ym_* and _ga* kind are used. Cookie lifetime: Metrica — up to 12 months, Google Analytics — up to 2 years; event data in Google Analytics is kept for 2 months. Session recording (Webvisor) and advertising pixels are not used. If you decline, these services are not loaded.

You can accept or decline cookies in the banner on your first visit. You can change your decision or withdraw consent at any time via the “Cookie settings” link at the bottom of the Website; you can also delete cookies in your browser settings.
