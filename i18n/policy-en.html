Measures applied to protect the User’s personal information

The Company takes the necessary and sufficient legal, organizational and technical measures to protect the User’s personal information from unlawful or accidental access, destruction, modification, blocking, copying, distribution, and other unlawful actions by third parties.   1. Processing of personal data using automation tools means performing actions (operations) on such data with the help of computing equipment in the Organization’s computer network (hereinafter, the CNO). The security of personal data during processing in the CNO is ensured by a personal data protection system comprising organizational measures and information protection tools, as well as the information technologies used in the CNO. Technical and software information protection tools must meet the requirements established in accordance with the legislation of the Republic of Belarus. Information protection tools used in the CNO undergo conformity assessment in the prescribed manner.
  2. Persons are admitted to processing personal data using automation tools by order of the Organization’s director and with access passwords. Work with personal data contained in the CNO is carried out in accordance with the “Instruction on information processing on information system resources and ensuring information security for company employees”, with which an employee whose duties include personal data processing is familiarized against signature.
  3. Work with personal data in the CNO must be organized so that the safety of personal data carriers and information protection tools is ensured, and uncontrolled presence of outsiders in these premises is excluded.
  4. Computers and (or) electronic folders containing files with personal data must be protected by individual access passwords for each user.
  5. Transmitting personal data over public communication networks, including the Internet, without special protection tools is prohibited.
  6. When processing personal data in the CNO, users must ensure:
    • use of the designated sections (directories) of information carriers built into technical equipment, or of removable labelled carriers;
    • no physical impact on the technical means of automated personal data processing that could disrupt their operation;

    • no unauthorized removal of equipment from the premises, installation or connection of equipment, or deletion, installation or configuration of software.
  7. When processing personal data in the CNO, developers and administrators of information systems must ensure:
    • training of persons using the information protection tools applied in the CNO in the rules for working with them;
    • accounting of persons admitted to work with personal data in the CNO, of access rights and passwords;
    • accounting of the information protection tools used and the operational and technical documentation for them;
    • monitoring compliance with the conditions for using information protection tools provided for by the operational and technical documentation;

  8. Specific requirements for personal data protection in individual automated systems of the Organization are determined by instructions for their use and operation approved in the prescribed manner.
